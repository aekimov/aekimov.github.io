Plainy helps you plan your day in seconds. Speak naturally, and the app turns your sentence into a set of timed tasks added to your calendar. You can mark items done, expand the calendar to see the week, and tap any task to tweak details like date, time, repetition, or reminders.

## Today at a glance

See what’s on your plate, mark items done, or expand to the month view for quick planning.
